1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PKR Cash Counter & Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Our application is designed with a strict offline-first architecture, meaning your calculations, cash balances, and financial records never leave your mobile device.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data)

We do not collect any personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use the app.
  • No Financial Account Linking: The app does not connect to bank accounts, debit/credit cards, mobile wallets (Easypaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, Nayapay), or external payment gateways.
  • No Usage Analytics or Tracking: We do not include third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Facebook Audience Network), or analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics or Mixpanel).

3. Local Storage on Your Device

Any data entered into the application—such as note counts (Rs. 5000, 1000, 500, 100, 75, 50, 20, 10), coin counts, target totals, customer change calculations, and saved cash slips—is stored locally in your device's private application sandbox using an embedded SQLite / Android Room database.

  • This local data is isolated by the Android OS and cannot be accessed by other applications.
  • You can edit, clear, or permanently delete any saved slip at any time within the app.
  • If you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android Settings, all locally stored records are permanently erased from your device.

4. Device Permissions

The application operates with zero dangerous or sensitive Android permissions. It does NOT request or require:

  • NO Camera permission
  • NO Microphone permission
  • NO Location permission (GPS/Network)
  • NO Contacts or Call Logs permission
  • NO External Media / Storage permissions (Photos, Videos, Audio)
  • NO Phone State or Hardware ID permissions

5. Sharing Features (User-Initiated Only)

The app includes an optional "Share Slip" feature that lets you export formatted cash breakdowns as text (for example, to WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram, or Email). This feature:

  • Is triggered exclusively by your manual action when you tap the Share button.
  • Uses the native Android System Share Sheet (Intent.ACTION_SEND).
  • Does not transmit data to any background server operated by us. You have full discretion over whom you share the text with.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

The app does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or behavioral trackers. If you choose to share your cash slip via external applications (e.g., messaging apps), the privacy policies of those respective services will govern the handling of that shared text.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children or any other users. The app is a non-invasive mathematical utility safe for all age groups.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any changes will be published at this link with an updated "Last Updated" date. Because our app does not collect user contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically.
