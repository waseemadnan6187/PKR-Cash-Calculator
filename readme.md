## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy governs your use of **PKR Cash Counter & Calculator**, an offline utility application designed for cashiers, shopkeepers, accountants, and general users in Pakistan to tally Pakistani Rupee currency notes and coins.

We prioritize your privacy above all else. Because we believe personal financial data should remain strictly confidential, the application does not connect to any external cloud backend or telemetry server.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process
### No Personal Information
We do not ask for, collect, or store personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or device identifiers.

### No Financial Account Information
The application does not link to any banking institutions, credit/debit cards, or Pakistani mobile payment systems (Easypaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay, NayaPay, etc.). It is purely an offline mathematical calculation and tallying tool.

### No Analytics or Advertising Trackers
We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) or user tracking frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK).

---

## 3. Local Data Storage & User Control
Any data you enter into the app—including denomination counts (Rs. 5000, 1000, 500, 100, 75, 50, 20, 10), coin tallies, custom labels, target amounts, and saved slips—is stored locally in your device's internal private app storage via an Android Room database.

- **Isolation:** This data is sandboxed by the Android operating system and is never accessible to other apps.
- **User Control:** You can reset counters with one tap or delete individual saved slips anytime.
- **Complete Deletion:** Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.

---

## 4. Device Permissions
The application does **not** request or use any sensitive or dangerous Android permissions:
- ❌ No `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`
- ❌ No `CAMERA` or `RECORD_AUDIO`
- ❌ No `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`
- ❌ No `READ_CONTACTS` or `READ_PHONE_STATE`

---

## 5. User-Initiated Data Sharing
When you choose to use the "Share Slip" function, the application formats your cash count into a plain text breakdown and launches Android's standard system share menu (`Intent.ACTION_SEND`). 

- The data is sent only to the recipient or app (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, or SMS) that you manually choose.
- No data passes through our servers.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, it is safe for all audiences.
